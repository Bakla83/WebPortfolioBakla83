About & Approach

A lifelong view of urological care.

Adolescent, paediatric and reconstructive urologist in Antwerp and London, working at the meeting point of surgery, development and wellbeing.

Biography

Gunter De Win is Associate Professor at the University of Antwerp and consultant in adolescent, paediatric and reconstructive urology at Antwerp University Hospital (UZA), his permanent academic and clinical base. He also holds a part-time consultant role in congenital lifelong urology at University College London Hospitals (UCLH).

He studied medicine at KU Leuven and trained in urology in Belgium and the Netherlands, obtaining his specialist qualification through Leuven. His PhD at KU Leuven (2014) focused on the development and implementation of a preclinical laparoscopic training programme.

Fellowships followed at UCLH (adolescent urology) and Great Ormond Street Hospital (paediatric urology) in London, together with repeated training visits in urethral reconstruction at the Kulkarni Urethroplasty Centre in Pune, India.

Since 2024 he has been programme director of the ESPU–FEAPU international training centre for paediatric urology at UZA.

The approach

Holistic and integrated.

Physical function is considered together with development, sexual wellbeing, fertility and daily life – and care continues as young people move from paediatric to adult services.

  1. 01

    Continuity

    Congenital conditions followed through puberty and into adulthood, with a structured transition between teams.

  2. 02

    The whole person

    Attention to sexual wellbeing, fertility and psychosocial development alongside surgical outcomes.

  3. 03

    Collaboration

    Working with colleagues in paediatrics, sexology, psychology, nursing and urotherapy.

  4. 04

    Evidence

    Clinical decisions informed by research into long-term outcomes.

Clinical work

Areas of expertise.

01

Congenital & lifelong urology

Hypospadias, exstrophy–epispadias and other congenital conditions, and the transition to adult care.

02

Urethral reconstruction

Male and female urethral stricture disease, including glans-preserving and endoscopic techniques.

03

Genital & bladder reconstruction

Reconstructive surgery, including complex and previously operated cases.

04

Ketamine uropathy

Recognition and management of urological damage related to ketamine use.

05

Sexual & voiding dysfunction

Function and wellbeing in adolescents and young adults.

06

Adolescent development

Testicular development, varicocele and urethral development during puberty.
