Speaking & Teaching

Lectures, live surgery and discussion.

Invited lectures, keynotes, live surgical demonstrations, masterclasses and panels – for specialist audiences and the wider public.

Topics

  1. Congenital lifelong urology & transition of carePreparing paediatric patients for adult life; adult outcomes of childhood conditions; setting up a transitional urology service.
  2. Urethral reconstruction & complex hypospadiasDistal and glandular strictures, failed hypospadias repairs, salvage surgery and endoscopic techniques.
  3. Surgical education & simulationStructured laparoscopic training, self-regulated learning and transfer of skills to the operating room.
  4. Young people’s sexual wellbeingResearch perspectives from the SWYPPE consortium for clinicians, educators and the public.
  5. Sexuality and fertility in congenital conditionsIncluding spina bifida, exstrophy–epispadias and differences of sex development.
  6. Ketamine uropathyClinical picture, management and public awareness.
  7. Digital urotherapy & sustainable surgeryHome uroflowmetry, digital tools and reusable operating-room textiles.

Glans-preserving buccal mucosa urethroplasty

An approach for glandular and distal urethral strictures that aims to preserve the structure of the glans. Published in the Turkish Journal of Urology (2022).

Endo-laparoscopic VUAS repair

A combined endoscopic–laparoscopic buccal mucosa technique for vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis. Best Educational Video, SIU 2023.
