Research

Questions that follow patients over time.

Research within the ASTARC group at the University of Antwerp, with partners in Belgium and abroad.

Consortium coordinator · 2024 – 2028 SWYPPE

Sexual Wellbeing of Young People in Times of Widespread Pornography Use

An interuniversity, interdisciplinary research consortium (FWO-SBO) bringing together KU Leuven, Ghent University, VUB, PXL and the University of Antwerp. It studies how young people’s sexual development and wellbeing relate to the wide availability of online pornography – combining medical, psychological, social and educational perspectives.

Themes

Research themes.

Long-term outcomes of congenital conditions

Sexual wellbeing, fertility and quality of life in adults who had urological surgery as children – including hypospadias, bladder exstrophy–epispadias and differences of sex development – and the long-term risks of childhood surgery.

Surgical education & simulation

Preclinical laparoscopic training, optimal training frequency, self-regulated versus instructor-led learning, and transfer of skills from simulation into clinical practice – the subject of his PhD and of continuing collaborations, including with Aarhus University.

Digital urotherapy & home uroflowmetry

Home flow measurement, digital bladder diaries, wearable bladder sensors and personalised urotherapy for children with lower urinary tract conditions (VLAIO Baekeland project, 2022 – 2026).

Adolescent testicular & urethral development

Testicular asymmetry and varicocele during puberty, and urethral development in adolescence.

Urethral reconstruction

Buccal mucosa techniques, inflammatory and idiopathic stricture disease, and endoscopic repair of bladder neck stenosis and bulbar strictures (IOF projects, 2024 – 2026).

Sustainable operating rooms

Reducing waste and supporting the adoption of reusable surgical textiles, including the reCURE project (VLAIO TETRA, 2022 – 2024) and doctoral research in product design.
