About

Seeing the whole person, across a whole life.

Gunter De Win is an adolescent, paediatric and reconstructive urologist. He is Associate Professor at the University of Antwerp and consultant at Antwerp University Hospital (UZA), his permanent academic and clinical base, with a part-time consultant role in congenital lifelong urology at University College London Hospitals.

His clinical work covers male and female urethral strictures, hypospadias and other congenital conditions, genital and bladder reconstruction, ketamine uropathy, and sexual and voiding dysfunction – with particular attention to the transition from childhood to adult care.

He trained in medicine and urology through KU Leuven, where he completed a PhD in surgical education, followed by fellowships at UCLH and Great Ormond Street Hospital, and training in urethral reconstruction at the Kulkarni Urethroplasty Centre in Pune, India.

His approach is holistic and integrated: physical function is considered together with development, sexual wellbeing, fertility and daily life, in collaboration with colleagues from other disciplines.

Research

Research within the ASTARC group at the University of Antwerp, with partners in Belgium and abroad.

Coordinator · 2024 – 2028

SWYPPE

Sexual Wellbeing of Young People in Times of Widespread Pornography Use

An interuniversity research consortium funded through FWO-SBO, with KU Leuven, Ghent University, VUB, PXL and the University of Antwerp. Medical, psychological, social and educational researchers study how sexual development and wellbeing relate to widespread online pornography.

Long-term outcomes of congenital conditions

Sexual wellbeing, fertility and quality of life in adults who had urological surgery as children.

Surgical education & simulation

Preclinical laparoscopic training, self-regulated versus instructor-led learning, and transfer of skills into practice – the subject of his PhD.

Digital urotherapy & home uroflowmetry

Home flow measurement, digital bladder diaries, wearable bladder sensors and personalised urotherapy for children.

Adolescent testicular & urethral development

Testicular asymmetry and varicocele during puberty, and urethral development.

Urethral reconstruction techniques

A glans-preserving buccal mucosa technique for distal strictures, and endo-laparoscopic repair of bladder neck stenosis.

Sustainable operating rooms

Reducing waste and adopting reusable surgical textiles, including the reCURE project (2022 – 2024).
