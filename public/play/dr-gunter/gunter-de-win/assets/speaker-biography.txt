MEDIUM (approx. 120 words)
Gunter De Win is an adolescent, paediatric and reconstructive urologist, Associate Professor at the University of Antwerp and consultant at Antwerp University Hospital (UZA). He is also a part-time consultant in congenital lifelong urology at University College London Hospitals (UCLH).

He trained in medicine and urology through KU Leuven, where he completed a PhD in surgical education, followed by fellowships at UCLH and Great Ormond Street Hospital in London and urethral reconstruction training at the Kulkarni Urethroplasty Centre in Pune, India.

His clinical work covers urethral strictures, hypospadias and other congenital conditions, genital and bladder reconstruction, ketamine uropathy and sexual and voiding dysfunction, with a holistic approach to adolescents and young adults. He coordinates SWYPPE, an interuniversity research consortium on young people's sexual wellbeing.
