The approach

Holistic and integrated – in four words.

L

Lifelong

Congenital conditions followed through puberty and into adult life, with careful transition from paediatric to adult care.

I

Integrative

Physical function considered together with development, sexuality, fertility and psychosocial wellbeing – alongside other disciplines.

F

Functional

Reconstruction aimed at how the body works in everyday life: voiding, sexual function and quality of life.

E

Evidence-based

Clinical practice informed by research into long-term outcomes, surgical education and new techniques.

About

Clinician, researcher and teacher.

Gunter De Win is an adolescent, paediatric and reconstructive urologist. He is Associate Professor at the University of Antwerp and consultant at Antwerp University Hospital (UZA), his permanent academic and clinical base, and part-time consultant in congenital lifelong urology at University College London Hospitals (UCLH).

He studied medicine and trained in urology through KU Leuven, where he also completed a PhD in surgical education. He then completed fellowships at UCLH and Great Ormond Street Hospital, and trained in urethral reconstruction at the Kulkarni Urethroplasty Centre in Pune, India.

Research

Asking the questions that matter later.

Consortium coordinator · 2024 – 2028

SWYPPE

Sexual Wellbeing of Young People in Times of Widespread Pornography Use

An interuniversity, interdisciplinary consortium (FWO-SBO) with KU Leuven, Ghent University, VUB, PXL and the University of Antwerp. Researchers from medicine, psychology, social and educational sciences study how young people’s sexual development and wellbeing relate to widely available online pornography.
