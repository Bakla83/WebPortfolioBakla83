01 — About & Approach

The person, not only the condition.

Urological conditions that begin in childhood rarely end there. Gunter De Win’s work follows young people through adolescence and into adult life – looking at function, development, sexual wellbeing and everyday life together.

He is Associate Professor at the University of Antwerp and consultant in adolescent, paediatric and reconstructive urology at Antwerp University Hospital (UZA), his permanent academic and clinical base. He also holds a part-time consultant role in congenital lifelong urology at University College London Hospitals (UCLH).

He studied medicine and trained in urology through KU Leuven, where he completed a PhD in surgical education. Fellowships followed at UCLH and Great Ormond Street Hospital in London, together with training visits in urethral reconstruction at the Kulkarni Urethroplasty Centre in Pune, India.

An integrated approach

  • Care that spans the transition from paediatric to adult services
  • Attention to sexual wellbeing, fertility and psychosocial development
  • Close collaboration with colleagues across disciplines
  • Clinical decisions grounded in research and long-term outcomes

03 — Research

Questions that follow patients over time.

Research at the University of Antwerp (ASTARC) spans long-term outcomes, surgical education, digital care and sustainable practice.

Consortium coordinator · 2024 – 2028 SWYPPE

Sexual Wellbeing of Young People in Times of Widespread Pornography Use

An interuniversity, interdisciplinary research consortium (FWO-SBO) bringing together KU Leuven, Ghent University, VUB, PXL and the University of Antwerp. It studies how young people’s sexual development and wellbeing relate to the wide availability of online pornography – combining medical, psychological, social and educational perspectives.

Research enquiries

Long-term outcomes

Sexual wellbeing, fertility and quality of life after congenital urological conditions and childhood surgery.

Surgical education

Simulation-based laparoscopic training, self-regulated learning and transfer of skills into clinical practice.

Digital urotherapy

Home uroflowmetry, digital bladder diaries and personalised urotherapy for children and adults.

Adolescent development
